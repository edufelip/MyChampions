# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students managing nutrition, training, hydration, and adherence, and professionals managing student relationships and care plans. They use MyChampions both on the move and during focused planning sessions at a desk.

## Product Purpose

MyChampions provides one consistent place to create, receive, follow, and review nutrition and training plans. Success means users can complete the same governed workflows on Android, iOS, and responsive web without losing role boundaries, offline safeguards, accessibility, or confidence in their data.

## Brand Personality

Athletic, optimistic, and focused. The interface should feel capable and encouraging without becoming loud, playful, or decorative.

## Anti-references

- Generic fitness apps dominated by neon gradients, gamified decoration, or body-transformation pressure.
- Desktop dashboards that expose every control at once and obscure the primary workflow.
- Mobile interfaces merely stretched across a large browser viewport.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user’s current task and care context obvious.
- Preserve one information architecture while adapting navigation and density to each input mode.
- Use familiar platform behavior and reserve emphasis for decisions and state.
- Make offline, read-only, entitlement, and error states explicit without blocking safe reading.
- Prefer consistent reusable primitives over screen-specific decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core flows must preserve readable contrast, dynamic text behavior, meaningful labels, logical focus order, keyboard operation on web, screen-reader announcements, and reduced-motion preferences. Health and fitness language must remain supportive and avoid shame-based framing.
